Entering a round

Pick the course, date and 9 holes, 18 holes or 9 + 9, then tap Done. Your next round starts at the last course you played.

Full entry has a card for each part of the hole: tee shot, approach, chipping, then putts and score. Each card turns green with a tick when it’s done. Quick entry asks only for score, putts, fairway and green.

Tap Next to move on. On the last hole it becomes Save round. Use Picked up if you didn’t finish a hole, Clear hole if you scored the wrong one, and Undo at the bottom to step back.

Courses and tee boxes

Each course keeps its par and stroke index (SI) for every tee box: Black, Blue, White, Red and Yellow. Pick your tee under the course name. A tee you haven’t set up yet starts as a copy of your first tee.

To change a course’s par or SI, tap More options then Edit pars & SI.

Course library

Tap Find a course online to search courses other golfers have shared and import one with all its tee boxes. You can share courses you’ve set up, report a problem with one, or send a tee you’ve added (under More options). Only the course details are shared, never your scores.

9 + 9 rounds

Play two 9-hole courses as one 18, such as Cootha then City. Tap 9 + 9, pick the front 9 and back 9 and a tee for each.

Harder 9: the harder 9 gets the odd stroke indexes (1, 3, 5…) and the other gets the even ones, so handicap shots go to the right holes. Check the club scorecard if you’re not sure.

Each 9 counts towards its own Eclectic medal and 9-hole records, and the whole round counts as an 18.

Handicap, Shots and Stableford

Enter your daily handicap in Hcp. For 9-hole rounds, use your 9-hole handicap. Once every hole has a stroke index, each hole shows a Shots box: the handicap shots you get there.

Your net score is strokes minus shots. Stableford points: 2 for a net par, 3 for a net birdie, 1 for a net bogey, and so on.

Stats

Overview shows where you’re losing shots and what to practise. Long game covers tee shots and approaches, and Short game covers chipping and putting. Filter by course or date at the top. The Holes tab shows how you play each hole.

Trophies and Eclectic medals

The Trophy cabinet keeps your personal records, such as best score, fewest putts and longest par streak. Beat one and you win a trophy when you save the round.

Eclectic is your best score on each hole at a course, across every round there. Bogey or better on every hole earns Bronze, par Silver, birdie Gold and eagle Platinum. The grid shows which holes you still need.

Backups and your coach

Your rounds are kept only on this phone. In History, tap Export backup now and then and save the file somewhere safe. Use Import file to restore it, or to move to a new phone.

Export CSV opens in a spreadsheet. The coach report (on Stats) makes a tidy PDF to send your coach before a lesson.

Free rounds and unlocking

Your first 3 rounds are free. After that, a one-time purchase of A$4.99 unlocks the app for good, with no subscription and no ads. On a new phone, tap Restore purchase.
