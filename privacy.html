Golf Stats Tracker

Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version

  • Your rounds, scores, stats and handicap stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The only thing that goes online is the optional course library: course details you choose to share, reports you choose to send, and a random code made by the app to stop spam.
  • You can ask us to delete anything you shared. See how to delete your data.

Who we are

Golf Stats Tracker (listed as “Golf Stats Tracker: Scorecard” on Google Play) is made by Rob Kift, trading as Kifty Productions (“Kifty” on Google Play), in Brisbane, Australia. In this policy, “we” means Kifty Productions.

Contact: kiftyproductions@gmail.com

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter while playing is stored only in the app on your phone: rounds, scores, clubs, putts, penalties, stats, trophies, your handicap, saved courses, your bag and the name you put on the coach report. We can’t see any of it.

If you delete the app (or clear its data), all of this is deleted from your phone. Keep a backup first if you want it: the app can export a backup file, which goes wherever you choose to save or send it.

When you share a round, a scorecard or a coach report, the app uses your phone’s normal share menu. It goes only to the app or person you pick; it doesn’t pass through us.

The course library (optional)

The course library lets golfers share the par and stroke index of a course, and load courses that others have shared. The app only goes online for it when you:

  • search the library or import a course,
  • share a course,
  • report a problem with a course, or
  • open the app after importing library courses (it checks whether any of them have been corrected).
When you…We receiveWho can see it
Search or importThe words you search for, or which courses to check for correctionsNot stored
Share a courseCourse name, suburb or town, state, country, par and stroke index for each hole, and the app’s random codeThe course details are public in the library. The random code is never shown.
Report a problemWhich course, the reason you pick, any note you type, and the random codeOnly us

The random code is created by the app on your phone the first time you share a course or send a report. It isn’t connected to your name, your Apple or Google account, your device’s advertising ID or anything else about you. We use it only to prevent spam and abuse, for example limiting how many courses one phone can share in a day, and allowing one report per phone per course.

The suburb, state and country describe where the course is. The app never uses your location.

Please don’t put personal details (yours or anyone else’s) in report notes.

Where it’s stored

The course library is hosted by Supabase in Sydney, Australia, and everything is sent encrypted (HTTPS). Supabase only stores and serves the data for us; it doesn’t use it for its own purposes. Like most web services, it keeps short-term technical logs that include IP addresses. We don’t use these to identify anyone.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is bought through Google Play or the App Store. They handle the payment, and the app only learns whether the unlock has been bought. We never see your payment details.

How to delete your data

On your phone: delete the app, or clear its data in your phone’s settings. Everything the app stored is removed.

From the course library: email kiftyproductions@gmail.com with the name and suburb of the course you shared, or the course you reported. We’ll delete it, together with the random code stored with it, within 30 days and let you know.

What’s kept: nothing else. Reports are deleted once we’ve dealt with them. A course that has been corrected or confirmed by other golfers may stay in the library without any link to you, as course details aren’t personal information.

Children

The app doesn’t ask anyone for personal information. Children should ask a parent or guardian before sharing a course or sending a report. If you’re a parent and think your child has sent us something, email us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change what the app collects, we’ll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.
